The problem

Sept 1: two-thirds of Boston leases turn over in a single day.

38 tons of extra trash and 1,700 mattresses in one week of 2023. Most of it was fine.

65,000 rental scam reports in five years, aimed at renters just like us.

A move is fifteen tabs, four group chats and a spreadsheet nobody trusts. You don't need another app. You're already in a thread.

Sources: City of Boston, press reports for the 2023 week; FTC Consumer Sentinel, 2020 to mid-2025.

We asked people who had just moved.

  • What did you throw away because you ran out of time?
  • Who did you trust with money, and who did you chase?
  • What did you wish someone else had handled?

Their answers are why every Elf asks first.

It looks like one chat. It is four agents.

Each one has its own tools, rules and memory. Pick an agent, pick a thing it does, and watch the thread.

      The build.

      What's under it

      • Photon 2.0 iMessage, SMS and voice line
      • Claude Haiku to route, Sonnet to draft, vision for photos
      • Retell the onboarding phone call
      • Nessie the ledger and the plan records
      • Browserbase + Stagehand real-browser house hunt
      • Bun + TypeScript one monorepo, a package per agent
      • Supabase Postgres memory, deals, plans
      • Railway hosting, migrations on boot

      How we know it works

      • 614 automated tests, 640 on Postgres
      • ~190 human test rows in TESTING.md
      • 1 real Nessie sandbox, smoke-tested live
      • 0 actions without a yes
